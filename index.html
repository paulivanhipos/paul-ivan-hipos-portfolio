Hi! I'm Paul Ivan Hipos, a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science student from Bulan, Sorsogon. I build clean, responsive websites with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Bootstrap, and I've deployed web apps like SorSU Mart, an online campus store and entrepreneurship system.
